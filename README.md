# HR Analytics Dashboard 📊

An interactive HR Analytics Dashboard built using Microsoft Power BI to analyze employee attrition, workforce demographics, salary patterns, job roles, education, and employee feedback.

The dashboard helps HR teams understand **why employees are leaving**, which employee groups have higher attrition, and what factors may contribute to employee turnover.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to analyze employee attrition and identify important patterns across different workforce dimensions.

The dashboard provides insights into:

- Overall employee count and attrition
- Attrition rate
- Employee age groups
- Salary slabs
- Education background
- Years spent at the company
- Job roles
- Gender-based attrition
- Employee reviews and feedback
- Reasons for leaving
- Employee ratings out of 4

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Attrition by Age
Analyzed employee attrition across different age groups to identify which age categories experience the highest employee turnover.

### 2. Attrition by Salary Slab
Analyzed attrition across different salary ranges:

- Up to 5K
- 5K–10K
- 10K–15K
- 15K+

This helps identify whether lower compensation levels are associated with higher employee attrition.

### 3. Attrition by Education
Analyzed employee attrition based on educational background, including:

- Life Sciences
- Medical
- Marketing
- Technical Degree
- Other

### 4. Attrition by Years at Company
Analyzed employee turnover based on the number of years employees have spent with the organization.

This helps identify periods during an employee's career when attrition is more likely to occur.

### 5. Attrition by Job Role
Compared attrition across different job roles to identify positions with higher employee turnover.

### 6. Attrition by Gender
Analyzed the distribution of employee attrition between male and female employees.

### 7. Employee Reviews & Feedback
Included employee review analysis to understand:

- Reasons employees are leaving
- Employee feedback
- Ratings provided by employees
- Rating distribution out of 4

This provides a more qualitative perspective alongside the numerical HR metrics.
